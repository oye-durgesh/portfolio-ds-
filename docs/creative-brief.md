# MASTER CREATIVE + DEVELOPMENT PROMPT
# THE BOOK OF MY JOURNEY
## An immersive 3D pop-up autobiography and interactive portfolio for Durgesh Shukla

You are an elite creative studio combining the skills of:
- Creative director
- 3D art director
- Storybook illustrator
- Blender environment artist
- Interaction designer
- Cinematic motion designer
- Senior React / Three.js engineer
- UX writer
- Frontend performance engineer

Your task is to DESIGN AND BUILD an extraordinary interactive 3D portfolio website for Durgesh Shukla.

This is not a conventional portfolio.

This is not a resume website.

This is not a presentation deck.

This is not a game.

It is a cinematic, interactive, 3D autobiography presented as a beautiful physical pop-up storybook.

The book is the website. The pages are the navigation. The miniature worlds are the chapters. The objects inside the book reveal the actual work.

The recruiter should feel that they are opening a beautifully crafted book and discovering the life, personality, work and thinking of a real person.

The experience must be visually memorable, emotionally engaging, technically impressive and easy to navigate.

==================================================
PART 1 — THE CENTRAL CREATIVE IDEA
==================================================

PROJECT TITLE:

THE BOOK OF MY JOURNEY

SUBTITLE:

A life built one page at a time.

CORE MESSAGE:

"I don't have everything figured out. I just don't stop figuring things out."

The story follows a curious, friendly, slightly chubby Indian boy who grows up, travels between cities, enters college, experiences internships, discovers problems, experiments, fails, learns, takes responsibility and builds things.

The entire journey is represented by one continuous physical 3D book.

The book begins closed.

As the visitor scrolls, the book opens.

Its pages turn.

Miniature cities, railway tracks, buildings, roads, parks, offices and workshops physically unfold from the pages.

The camera moves through these miniature worlds.

The same character appears in every chapter, growing older while retaining the same recognisable face and personality.

The final page opens into an unfinished world representing the next chapter of my life.

The book should feel handcrafted, tactile, dimensional and magical.

==================================================

BOOK MATERIALS:

- Warm ivory paper.
- Slightly textured pages.
- Thick illustrated paper cutouts.
- Layered cardstock buildings.
- Visible paper folds.
- Embossed chapter titles.
- Subtle paper shadows.

- Cream.
- Forest green.
- Muted olive.
- Terracotta.

Use warm cinematic lighting.

A soft daylight environment should illuminate the pages.

Create subtle shadows beneath pop-up structures.

Use ambient occlusion, soft contact shadows, gentle depth of field and carefully controlled reflections.

Do not make the world overly shiny, plastic or toy-like.

Use an elegant editorial serif for chapter headings.

Use a clean modern sans-serif for body copy.
