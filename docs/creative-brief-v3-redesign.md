# Portfolio Redesign — Make the Open Book the Entire Experience

Redesign the existing portfolio while preserving its chapter-based personal storytelling concept. The open 3D book must become the central visual identity and primary content container.

Use the supplied open-book reference screenshot as the visual direction. Do not replace it with a dashboard, a collection of floating cards, a conventional portfolio layout or a generic website template.

## 1. Core visual design

- A large, realistic, open 3D book should occupy the centre of the desktop viewport.
- Use warm cream paper, visible page edges, a dark green book cover, a dark walnut background and restrained gold and rust accents.
- Use elegant editorial serif typography for chapter titles and reflections, paired with a clean sans-serif for body copy.
- The book should have convincing depth, perspective, shadows and page geometry.
- The illustrated 3D chapter environment should sit naturally on top of the open book, visually integrated with its pages.
- Maintain a polished, cinematic and premium appearance.

## 2. One element, one purpose

The two book pages must have distinct roles:

LEFT PAGE:
- Chapter number and location.
- Chapter title.
- A concise introductory paragraph.
- Optional small chapter category.

RIGHT PAGE:
- One meaningful reflection, insight or personal lesson.
- Additional supporting content only when it adds new information.

ABOVE THE BOOK:
- A contextual illustrated 3D miniature environment representing the chapter.
- The scene must visually communicate the actual experience.

Do not repeat the same title, description, quote, metric or paragraph in multiple places.

Remove the persistent left-side information card if it duplicates the book content. Remove generic "Read the full story" buttons. Do not create multiple cards containing the same information.

## 3. Same-screen story interaction

When a visitor selects a chapter or its interactive scene, show the detailed story in an elegant reading panel or modal on the same screen.

Do not navigate to a different page.

Keep the book visible behind the panel. The panel should feel like opening a deeper page of the same book.

Use a clear editorial structure:
- Context
- What I noticed
- What I did
- What went wrong (when relevant)
- What changed
- What I learned

Only include sections relevant to that chapter. Avoid repeating the chapter introduction. The panel should add depth, not duplicate the main screen.

## 4. Full India map travel transitions

Build a continuous animated geographical journey.

For major city transitions:
1. Pull the camera back from the current illustrated chapter.
2. Reveal a stylised India map.
3. Animate the route between the two locations.
4. Show an appropriate mode of transportation, such as a train or aeroplane.
5. Animate the journey along the route.
6. Zoom into the destination.
7. Reveal the destination-specific miniature environment.
8. Transition naturally into the next open-book chapter.

In particular, the Greater Noida to Bengaluru transition must show the map, route, transportation and arrival in Bengaluru. Do not simply switch the chapter scene.

The route animation should feel like one continuous journey.

## 5. BackWithBrain chapter

Introduce BackWithBrain accurately as an early-stage meditation and mind-fitness company.

Show the early-stage environment and the challenge of joining without a fixed job description.

Create a contextual miniature environment containing elements such as:
- Meditation and mind-fitness programme.
- From Chaos to Clarity seven-day programme.
- Customer research and feedback workspace.
- Product planning and research wall.
- Employee wellness and HR dashboard concepts.

Distinguish between features that were actually worked on, concepts that were explored and products that were launched. Never depict an idea as a shipped feature without evidence.

Tell the story through decisions, execution, experiments, setbacks and learning—not generic claims.

## 6. GoCattles and failure storytelling

Make the GoCattles environment visually engaging: premium dog food products, a small dog, content planning, competitor research, product listings and campaign workspace.

The Prime Day chapter should communicate the important lesson that attention and engagement do not automatically translate into customer demand or sales.

Explain what went wrong, what I learned, how I responded and what I carried forward. Do not imply that I alone caused the campaign outcome or the company's later closure.

Avoid cluttered dashboard-like statistic cards. Present verified results naturally in the chapter, with meaningful context.

## 7. Visual quality and motion

- Make the book the dominant focal point.
- Keep the layout balanced and readable.
- Avoid overlapping text and illustrations.
- Use smooth camera transitions, subtle scene animations and meaningful chapter changes.
- Provide a working Reduce Motion control.
- Ensure responsive layouts for smaller screens.
- Keep chapter navigation accessible without covering the book.
- Make every interaction functional, not decorative.

## 8. Content and quality rules

- No repeated content across the left page, right page, scene, sidebar and modal.
- No generic "Read the story" button.
- No unnecessary floating cards.
- No unsupported numbers, fake graphs or invented business outcomes.
- No static map between cities when the narrative requires travel.
- No generic company descriptions.
- No game mechanics, scores, leaderboards or artificial achievements.

The final experience should feel like an illustrated, cinematic autobiography in which the visitor explores a beautifully designed open book and travels through the important places, decisions, experiments, failures and projects that shaped my journey.

**The reference open-book screenshot is the visual standard. Build around that design rather than introducing another visual system.**
