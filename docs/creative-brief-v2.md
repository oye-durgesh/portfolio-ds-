# Build: The Book of My Journey — Durgesh Shukla

Build a premium, immersive, highly interactive 3D portfolio website for Durgesh Shukla.

This is a personal storytelling portfolio designed to demonstrate my journey, professional growth, high agency, ownership, ability to learn quickly, product thinking, growth mindset and ability to turn ideas into execution.

It is intended for the Wispr Flow team, particularly people evaluating me for a growth-oriented role.

The website must feel like an interactive storybook combined with a cinematic 3D world. It must not look like a traditional resume, a generic portfolio template, a game, or a collection of disconnected project cards.

Use the uploaded screenshots as visual references for the existing direction. Preserve the premium book aesthetic, dark wooden background, forest-green book cover, warm cream pages, gold detailing, serif typography, chapter navigation and miniature 3D environments. Improve the realism, readability, transitions, storytelling and scene quality substantially.

## 2. Visual experience and book interaction

### Opening screen — The cover

Create a beautiful, realistic, deep forest-green hardcover book placed on a dark wooden table.

The cover should contain:

- THE BOOK OF MY JOURNEY
- DURGESH SHUKLA
- A LIFE BUILT ONE PAGE AT A TIME
- A delicate gold-embossed route connecting Kanpur, Greater Noida and Bengaluru.

Use realistic materials: textured green fabric, gold foil lettering, thick cream-coloured pages, subtle shadows and cinematic lighting.

The book should occupy most of the screen.

When the user scrolls or enters the experience:
1. Camera slowly approaches the book.
2. The cover opens with a realistic page-turn animation.
3. The first spread becomes visible.
4. The camera moves into the chapter environment.

The book must remain visually consistent throughout the experience.

### Book layout

Every chapter should have a proper two-page spread.

**Left page:**
- Chapter number.
- Location or company.
- Chapter title.
- Short chapter introduction.
- One meaningful photograph or visual.
- Optional evidence or metrics where relevant.

**Right page:**
- Main story.
- What I experienced.
- What I noticed.
- What I did.
- What changed or what I learned.

Use a clear, readable type hierarchy.

The written content on the physical pages must be large enough to read on desktop and mobile. Do not use tiny decorative handwriting for important information.

The 3D environment must never cover important text.

### Camera and transition behaviour

Use a cinematic sequence:

1. Show the open book from a beautiful top-down camera angle.
2. Slowly zoom into the spread.
3. Transition into the chapter's 3D miniature environment.
4. Keep the book spread visible underneath or behind the environment as the narrative context.
5. As the visitor scrolls, animate the character and relevant objects.
6. At the end of the chapter, zoom back out to the book.
7. Turn the page and introduce the next chapter.

The book must feel like one continuous object, not separate slides.

### Navigation

Create a minimal fixed navigation bar:

- Durgesh Shukla
- The Book of My Journey
- Current chapter indicator
- Contents
- Jump to Work

The Contents button should open a clean chapter index. The visitor should be able to jump to a chapter without losing the overall experience.

Add previous and next chapter navigation.

Scrolling should be the primary interaction. Do not make every object clickable. Do not introduce game mechanics, points, badges, leaderboards or unnecessary interactions.
